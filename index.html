EasyABA

EasyABA creates ABA bank payment files, the standard bulk payment file Australian banks accept (also called a Cemtex file). Enter or upload who to pay and how much, download the file, and upload it to your bank to pay many suppliers or staff in one go.

  • Checks BSBs, account numbers and amounts before the file is made
  • Works with the major Australian banks' bulk payment upload
  • Nothing to install: it runs in your web browser

[$9.90] per month

Contact

Questions, help with your account or billing: email xeromys@gmail.com. We reply within two business days.

Billing

Subscriptions are billed monthly in Australian dollars, in advance, by card through Stripe. We never see or store your card details. You can change your plan, update your card or download your invoices at any time from Manage billing in your account.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from Manage billing in your account, or by emailing us. Your subscription stays active until the end of the month you have paid for, then stops; you will not be charged again.

Refunds and disputes

Because you can cancel at any time, we do not refund part-months. If something has gone wrong (you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or the product did not work as described) email us and we will put it right, including a refund where one is due.

Nothing here limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, including your right to a refund for a major failure.

If you do not recognise a charge, please contact us before disputing it with your bank; it will show on your statement as XEROMYS.

Privacy

We use the details you give us only to run your account and provide the product. We do not sell or share them. Payments are handled by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy.
